S2 · NACELLE
ROTATION CALIBRATION
  1. Line the nacelle up with the mark.
  2. Press START MEASUREMENT.
  3. Use LEFT / RIGHT to rotate the nacelle exactly one full turn.
  4. Press FINISH. The turn time is saved automatically.
FULL TURN TIME (AT 100%)--

Set the nacelle straight by hand (cables untwisted), then press ZERO POSITION. This resets the cable twist to 0.
